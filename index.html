adaptiveNet

Interactive demos of node–edge dynamical systems — networks where node states and edge weights coevolve under simple rules. Click a card to open the demo, drag the sliders, watch the emergence. See SPEC.md for the design.

Network Turing Patterns
Reaction–diffusion on a graph. When the inhibitor diffuses much faster than the activator, the network splits into high / low clusters. Hubs become pattern organizers.

Adaptive Voter (Holme–Newman)
Edges rewire when neighbors disagree; opinions copy when they don't. Echo chambers form above a critical rewire rate. The structure of the network co-evolves with the opinions on it.
open →
Gray–Scott on a 2D Grid
Reaction–diffusion on a square lattice. Spots, mazes, mitosis, worms — the same two equations produce wildly different patterns depending on (f, k).
open →
Classical Turing (Brusselator)
The original 1952 Turing mechanism on a 2D lattice. Two species, two diffusion rates; the homogeneous state goes unstable and stripes / spots appear from arbitrarily small noise. Same chemistry as the network Turing demo, different topology.
